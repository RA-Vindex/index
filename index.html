RA-V Index

RA-V Index is a curated repository compiling the dispersed writings, lectures, manifestos, technical papers, and theoretical fragments of Rodrigo Arellano-Voronov, Professor and independent Researcher in Structural Dynamics, Finance, and Inorganic Propagation.

Disclaimer

The RA-V Index does not present a unified doctrine. It maps an evolving research program that spans financial topology, infrastructural convergence, techno-convergence versus techno-diversity, systemic risk, and architectural governance. Its purpose is archival and cartographic rather than authoritative.

Many of the documents collected here represent only one available version—sometimes partial, revised, or reconstructed—of papers, monographs, lectures, and conference materials that have circulated across the web over the past fifteen to twenty years. Earlier drafts, alternate iterations, and unpublished variants may exist but are not always accessible.

Accordingly, the Index does not claim to provide a definitive or official account of Arellano-Voronov’s work. It assembles and preserves traceable materials in order to document the development of a structural theory of convergence in late modernity as it has emerged across dispersed platforms and formats.
